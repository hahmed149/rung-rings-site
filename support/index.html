Rung Rings: Support

Rung Rings is a calm puzzle game for iPhone. Turn the rings until the pattern lines up. Each of the five worlds borrows its colours and patterns from a living craft of Pakistan, and each has its own rule for how the rings behave.

Need help, found a bug, or have a thought? Write to hahmed149@outlook.com. A reply usually takes a few days.

The basics

How do I turn a ring?

Drag a ring around the centre, or flick it and let it spin. It settles on a notch. A turn that changes the ring counts as one move. A ring that settles back where it was does not count.

What do the stars mean?

Every level has a "par", the fewest moves that can solve it. Solve in par moves for three stars. One or two extra moves gives two stars, and finishing at all gives one. Using the Reveal hint caps a level at two stars. Your best result is kept: replaying never lowers it.

What are points?

Each level is also scored out of 1000 points. You lose 100 for every move over par, and hints and Undo cost points too (below). Points are a finer grain under the stars. They unlock nothing, there is no timer and nobody sees your score. Your best score per level is kept.

What is the thread (move limit)?

Some levels show a spool: the number of moves you may use. If you run out without solving, the rings gently unwind back to the start and you try again. Nothing is lost, there are no lives, and it costs no points.

Hints and points

Tap the Hint button to open the hint tray. You choose how much help you want:

  • Nudge (costs 100 points each time): shows which ring to turn.
  • Show (250 points each time): shows which ring, which way, and how many notches.
  • Reveal (500 points, once per attempt): ghosts the solved pose. Caps the level at two stars.
  • Undo (100 points each use): takes back your last move and gives the move (and thread) back.

Restarting a level starts a fresh attempt with all costs cleared. Hints are always optional, and the game never makes you buy anything.

The five worlds and their rules

  • World 1, Sindh Dawn (ajrak block printing, Sindh): free rings. Turn each ring until the print lines up.
  • World 2, Cholistan Nights (chunri tie-dye, Sindh and Bahawalpur): knots. Drag a knotted ring and both of its neighbours turn with it, live, by the same amount.
  • World 3, Punjab Garden (phulkari embroidery, the Punjab region): stepped rings. They turn only in whole steps of two notches, like counted stitches.
  • World 4, Thar Mirrors (mirror work and ralli quilts, Tharparkar): locks. A dull ring will not turn until the neighbour that holds its key is solved and at rest.
  • World 5, Highway Bazaar (truck art, Karachi and Rawalpindi): gears. Meshed rings turn together, in opposite directions.

Every rule is taught by a short tutorial the first time you meet it. You can replay any of them in Settings, under Tutorials.

The story

Nani's bundle: five unfinished cloths, each remembering where it came from. The story appears between levels, never during one, and nothing blocks play. You can replay any of it, and read every craft fact you have unlocked, from the Bundle button on the title screen or Settings, under Story.

iCloud sync

What it does. If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is also kept in your own iCloud key-value storage (Apple's servers, your Apple Account, unreadable to the developer). A new phone, or a reinstall, picks it up automatically.

Turning it on. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and make sure iCloud is on and that Rung Rings is allowed under apps using iCloud. Settings inside the game shows whether sync is on.

It is optional. Signed out of iCloud, the game works exactly the same, and progress stays on this device only.

Two devices. Progress from both devices is merged: the best stars and points per level win, and nothing is lost. Sync is handled by iOS in the background and can take a few minutes. Opening and closing the app helps.

Newer version on another device. If another device saved progress with a newer version of the app, Settings says "Update the app to sync", and your local progress is left alone.

Resetting your progress

Open Settings, tap Reset progress, then confirm in the second step. This clears levels, stars, points, tutorial and story flags on this device. If iCloud sync is on, the reset is recorded there too, so your other devices will not bring the old progress back. It cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes the on-device data; with iCloud on, reinstalling restores your progress from iCloud.

Sound, haptics and Reduce Motion

  • Silent switch. The game's sounds obey the silent switch and mix with other audio, so music you are playing carries on. Flip the switch to silent and the game is silent. Haptics still work unless you turn them off.
  • Sound and haptics can be turned off separately in Settings.
  • Reduce Motion. If Reduce Motion is on in iOS Accessibility settings, the game skips particles, idle spinning and parallax and fades story cards instead of sliding them. Nothing about the puzzles changes.

Reporting a bug

Please email hahmed149@outlook.com with:

  • the version shown at the bottom of Settings (for example "Rung Rings 1.3 (13)"),
  • your iPhone model and iOS version,
  • what you were doing, and the level (for example "World 3, level 7") if it is about a level.

A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot. The app does not send any reports itself, so we only know about a problem if you tell us.

Privacy

Rung Rings has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, collects no personal data and has no network code of its own. See the privacy policy.
