Ten Past: Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026.

Ten Past helps you limit the apps you choose, with Apple's Screen Time.

What it collects

Nothing. Ten Past has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. It sends nothing off your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

The apps you choose, their limits, and a small log of when limits were reached are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Apple gives Ten Past only opaque tokens for the apps you choose, not their names or what you do in them. Ten Past cannot see your messages, feeds, browsing or anything you do inside other apps.

Screen Time

Ten Past asks for Screen Time access so iOS can tell it when a limit is reached and lock the app. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings, Screen Time.

Deleting your data

Deleting Ten Past deletes everything it stored.

Children

Ten Past is meant for people managing their own iPhone and collects no data from anyone.
