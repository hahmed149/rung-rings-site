Ten Past: Support

Ten Past is a free iPhone app for people who can't put the phone down. Each app you choose gets a few minutes an hour, or a cap for the day. When the time is used, the app locks until the next hour, or until 6 AM for a daily limit.

Need help, found a bug, or have a thought? Write to hahmed149@outlook.com. A reply usually takes a few days.

How it works

How do I set it up?

Open Ten Past, allow Screen Time (Apple's screen, confirmed with Face ID), choose the apps you scroll too much, and set each one's limit. Every app starts at 10 minutes an hour.

Each hour or each day?

Each app has one limit. Each hour: say 10 minutes, then it locks until the next hour starts. Each day: say 1 hour in all, then it locks until 6 AM.

What rolls over?

For an hourly limit, minutes you did not use carry into the next hour, up to 5. Use 3 of 10 at 2 PM and 3 PM allows 15.

Why can't I raise a limit right away?

Lower a limit and it applies now. Raise one, or switch it, and it waits until 6 AM, so a limit can't be loosened while you are hitting it.

What is the one hour pass?

When you go to remove a locked app, Ten Past offers to unlock it for one hour instead. You get one pass every 3 days. Or you can remove the app from Ten Past if you have made up your mind, and it unlocks at once.

Where did my app go?

A locked app is off your Home Screen until it unlocks. Its website still opens in Safari. If an app stays missing after its time, open Ten Past: it checks itself on open. In Settings, "Bring back hidden apps" unlocks everything at once.

Turning it off

Remove an app in Ten Past's Settings to unlock it. To stop everything, delete Ten Past, or turn off its Screen Time access in iOS Settings, Screen Time.
